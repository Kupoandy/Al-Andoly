Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes AL ANDOLY different from regular perfumes?

AL ANDOLY is 100% alcohol-free, oil-based, and handcrafted using traditional Middle Eastern methods. Our perfumes are significantly more concentrated and long-lasting than standard eau de parfum, offering 24+ hours of wear from minimal application.

Is AL ANDOLY suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes! Because we use no harsh alcohol and only premium oils, AL ANDOLY is ideal for sensitive skin. The oil-based formula is gentle while still delivering powerful fragrance presence.

How long does one bottle last?

Due to the concentrated formula, one 6ml bottle can last 2-3 months with regular daily use (2-3 applications). Your bottle becomes a daily companion, not a quick consumable.

Can I use AL ANDOLY on clothing and body?

Absolutely! Our oil-based formula works beautifully on skin, clothing, and even fabrics. Be mindful that oils may leave subtle marks on certain delicate fabrics, so test on inconspicuous areas first.

How do I store AL ANDOLY properly?

Store in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight. The amber glass bottles protect the oil from UV damage. Avoid extreme temperatures. Properly stored, AL ANDOLY maintains quality for years.
